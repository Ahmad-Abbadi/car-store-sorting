Title: Toy Car Sort
Launch: Basic
Engine: Unity 6
Devices: Desktop browsers
Orientation: Landscape
Multiplayer: No
Progress save: PlayerPrefs / CrazyGames Automatic Progress Save

Description:
Turn a cluttered toy car shop into a collector's paradise! Explore the store in first person, pick up miniature cars, and sort them onto matching themed shelves. Learn the basics in a guided tutorial, discover colorful collections, complete collector quests, and watch your displays come together one car at a time.

Controls:
WASD or arrow keys: Move
Mouse: Look around
Left click or E: Primary interaction
Right click, F or Q: Secondary interaction
Mouse wheel: Cycle held items
Hold middle mouse button: Inspect a held car
Space: Jump
Left Shift: Sprint
C: Collection
1 / 2: Collection powers
Tab or Escape: Pause

Reviewer notes:
New players are offered a language selection and a guided hands-on tutorial before entering the shop. Progress is stored using Unity PlayerPrefs. The game includes the CrazyGames SDK and reports gameplay start/stop; no ads or purchases are requested.
