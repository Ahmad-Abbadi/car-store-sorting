# Tiny Riders — Phase A0 family visual summary and 50-base roster

This roster follows the stable IDs and silhouette briefs in [bodies_50.json](../../Tiny_Riders_500_Car_Production_Kit/catalog/bodies_50.json), using the ten original sheets for color and shape language. Each name below is one **base body**, not a livery. Each later receives a standard and visibly different custom physical build, then five printed editions per build.

## 01 City Crew (`CC`)

Bright blue city backgrounds, yellow accents, friendly square faces, upright cabins, and practical little vehicles. Featured reference: red Pocket. Palette start: `#287DCA` / `#FFC847` / `#144A89`.

- `CC-01` **Pocket** — ultra-short mini hatch with square face and near-upright windscreen.
- `CC-02` **Bean** — tall narrow rounded microcar with almost no bonnet.
- `CC-03` **Parcel** — short delivery van with high cube cargo section.
- `CC-04` **Metro** — flat-front people-carrier or taxi with raised box roof.
- `CC-05` **Loop** — tall square microbus per catalog; the sheet's rear-loop car conflicts and is flagged in the audit.

## 02 Track Club (`TC`)

Cobalt blue, orange, checker and speed graphics; compact racing bodies with big wings and wide tires. Featured Blue Racer #23 anchors the entire toy style. Palette start: `#1765D7` / `#FF831C` / `#123D8A`.

- `TC-01` **Blue Racer** — short broad blue coupe, slanted cabin, rectangular nose, strong rear wing.
- `TC-02` **Wedge** — pointed low racer with narrow cabin and long sloping hood.
- `TC-03` **Sprint** — upright small racing truck, square hood, wide rear deck.
- `TC-04` **Rocket** — elongated low dragster per catalog; the sheet's boxy olive car conflicts.
- `TC-05` **Apex** — wide low track coupe, simplified flared fenders and square lamp print.

## 03 Retro Rollers (`RR`)

Red, cream and mint diner nostalgia; round-square noses, older body profiles, simple chrome-colored plastic. Featured Diner. Palette start: `#D93938` / `#F6DBBC` / `#863D32`.

- `RR-01` **Diner** — stubby vintage coupe with upright windshield and rounded-square nose.
- `RR-02` **Longroof** — long square-window wagon with extended cargo roof.
- `RR-03` **Beach** — compact bug-like form with balloon fenders.
- `RR-04` **Chop** — short hot rod, tall grille, visibly exposed tires.
- `RR-05` **Comet** — boxy fastback with short trunk and sloping rear.

## 04 Dirt Patrol (`DP`)

Olive, sand and orange; higher stance, thick all-terrain tires, chunky trail gear and mountain motif. Featured Pebble. Palette start: `#576541` / `#F18B38` / `#313F35`.

- `DP-01` **Pebble** — short tall off-road buggy with upright cab and bumper.
- `DP-02` **Ridge** — lifted double-cab pickup with short open bed.
- `DP-03` **Dune** — open buggy, minimal cabin and large fenders.
- `DP-04` **Trail** — square-nosed short wagon with five rectangular windows.
- `DP-05` **Scout** — high box camper van with overland side blocks.

## 05 Big Wheels (`BW`)

Purple and chartreuse family graphics; toy bodies perched above dominant monster tires, thick guards and bold front bars. Featured orange Crusher. Palette start: `#52218C` / `#D9F94E` / `#28203F`.

- `BW-01` **Crusher** — small square pickup mounted high over enormous tires.
- `BW-02` **Blockade** — armored-looking box van on monster tires.
- `BW-03` **Stomper** — very short square 4×4 above massive wheels.
- `BW-04` **Mammoth** — monster truck with blocky toy dump body.
- `BW-05` **Scramble** — wide monster buggy, short cab and long nose.

## 06 Rescue Crew (`RC`)

Red, blue, white and gold service colors; simple beacons, robust bumpers, readable symbols as print. Featured red Flash. Palette start: `#2569C8` / `#EF3D3C` / `#163B77`.

- `RC-01` **Flash** — broad emergency patrol coupe with strong hood.
- `RC-02` **Medic** — compact ambulance with visibly taller box body.
- `RC-03` **Ladder** — short fire truck with rectangular equipment body.
- `RC-04` **Hook** — recovery truck carrying a rear tow assembly.
- `RC-05` **Harbor** — blue seaside rescue pickup with open bed.

## 07 Workyard (`WY`)

Construction yellow and charcoal with orange/teal machinery accents; equipment creates deliberately distinct silhouettes. Featured Tipper. Palette start: `#EBAE21` / `#283B49` / `#F47E28`.

- `WY-01` **Tipper** — square cab and open blocky dump bed.
- `WY-02` **Mixer** — small cab and simple cylindrical drum.
- `WY-03` **Lift** — compact forklift with driver cage and front tines.
- `WY-04` **Crane** — truck-mounted mobile crane with a thick boom.
- `WY-05` **Loader** — short tractor with a broad front bucket.

## 08 Snack Street (`SS`)

Cream, pink, mint and candy color; food associations should read from large actual roof forms and restrained printed detail. Featured Scoop. Palette start: `#EE94B8` / `#68B67D` / `#6A3146`.

- `SS-01` **Scoop** — rounded-front tiny ice-cream coupe on thick wheels.
- `SS-02` **Slice** — little wedge-shaped pizza delivery racer.
- `SS-03` **Burger** — short minivan with layered burger form.
- `SS-04` **Bento** — upright square food truck and cube cargo.
- `SS-05` **Donut** — rounded buggy with arch-like roof line.

## 09 Future Fleet (`FF`)

Blue, cyan and violet; planar sci-fi wedges, broad canopies, angular wings, simple sensor shapes. Featured Pixel. Palette start: `#2188DB` / `#A64BE2` / `#143985`.

- `FF-01` **Pixel** — low faceted hatch coupe with broad front and wedge cabin.
- `FF-02` **Orbit** — rounded capsule microcar with planar toy dome.
- `FF-03` **Volt** — flat electric-style pickup with tapered nose.
- `FF-04` **Rover** — six-wheel utility science explorer.
- `FF-05` **Vector** — pointed low hypercar with flat canopy.

## 10 Creature Cruisers (`CR`)

Playful blue, coral, green and purple creatures; friendly printed faces plus a few unmistakable physical fins, shells, horns or eye housings. Featured Shark. Palette start: `#167FD2` / `#F5684C` / `#172F78`.

- `CR-01` **Shark** — short blue car, pointed snout and dorsal fin.
- `CR-02` **Turtle** — low domed buggy with arched shell roof.
- `CR-03` **Dino** — square red truck with three triangular back plates.
- `CR-04` **Frog** — wide green buggy with two raised eye housings.
- `CR-05` **Dragon** — squat purple coupe with horns and short side wings.

**Count:** 10 families × 5 named and distinct base silhouettes = **50**. The listed colors are kit palette starting points; compare the final material/texture swatches against each source sheet during the family review.
