# Themes Expansion 01

 THE TWENTY NEW THEME DESIGNS

### 01 — WILD WEST
Proposed ID: TH_WILD_WEST

Palette:
Saddle brown #8B4F2C
Cream #F0D6A4
Denim #325B86
Brass #D9AD55

Car textures:
A large original sheriff-style star emblem, bandana panels, broad rope loops,
cactus silhouettes, restrained faux-leather stitching.
No text or numbers.

Primary model:
Carefully shaped cowboy hat with a pinched crown and rolled brim,
fitted closely to the roof or rear deck.

Optional support:
Compact tied rope coil secured to the rear.

Keep distinct from Pirate:
Recognizable cowboy brim, denim/bandana identity, no tricorn or skull emblem.


### 02 — DETECTIVE
Proposed ID: TH_DETECTIVE

Palette:
Tan #C69C72
Dark cocoa #4A3329
Cream #F1E6D2
Burgundy #762D42

Car textures:
Oversized magnifying-glass emblem, large readable plaid sections,
footprint trails and simple fingerprint-like spirals.

Primary model:
A shaped deerstalker-style cap with front/rear brims and folded ear flaps.

Optional support:
Short chunky magnifying glass resting securely on the rear deck.
Its lens can be opaque stylized material.

Keep distinct from Cowboy:
Double-brim cap and investigation motifs, not a recolored wide-brim hat.


### 03 — NINJA
Proposed ID: TH_NINJA

Palette:
Charcoal #18212B
Crimson #CF3445
Ivory #EEE7DB
Muted steel #70808D

Car textures:
Bold cloth-wrap panels, a stylized eye-band motif, angular wind strokes,
and large original folded-paper/shuriken-style emblems painted flat.

Primary model:
A sculpted headband-like roof wrap with a knot and two short,
thick swept-back fabric tails.

Optional support:
A compact tied scroll case.

Keep the windows unobstructed.

Do not add weapons, long cloth strips, mesh symbols,
or just repaint the Pirate hat.


### 04 — VIKING LEGENDS
Proposed ID: TH_VIKING_LEGENDS

Palette:
Slate #46515F
Warm tan #B79068
Bronze #BE8849
Ice-blue accent #A4CDDE

Car textures:
Large round-shield artwork, broad knotwork borders, wood-grain blocks,
and printed rivet patterns.

Primary model:
An original fantasy Norse helmet with broad cheek guards and two short
swept-back horn shapes.

Build it as a stylized toy, not a historical reconstruction.

Optional support:
One small round physical shield on a secure rear mount.
Its design is printed.

Keep distinct from Royal/Knight:
Wood, knotwork, and horned silhouette rather than crown/gem decoration.


### 05 — ANCIENT EGYPT
Proposed ID: TH_ANCIENT_EGYPT

Palette:
Sandstone #D8B577
Deep lapis #174A87
Turquoise #2BBDB7
Gold #E8BC48

Car textures:
Broad blue/gold headdress bands, large scarab and eye emblems,
geometric lotus borders, simple pyramid silhouettes.

Primary model:
A stylized nemes-inspired headdress cap with chunky side flaps fitted

### 09 — BUTTERFLY GARDEN

Mint #83C9B3
Peach #F3AA87
Cream #FFF2DB

Pale gray #D9E3EC
Electric yellow #FFD342
Rain blue #71BBE3

Coral #ED6558

### 20 — SCIENCE LAB
Proposed ID: TH_SCIENCE_LAB

Palette:
Mint #79CBB4
Violet #7754A7
Lime #C8E76B
Off-white #F0F1E4

Car textures:
Large flask/bubble illustrations, broad molecular-bond motifs
and clean laboratory panel shapes.

No formula lettering or numerical markings.

Primary model:
A recognizable conical flask with a short narrow neck and stable fitted base.

Two short capped test tubes in one low holder.

Flask silhouette and laboratory symbols, no saucer, ringed planet,
or alien face.
