Downloaded 2026-10-17. Original published sources are Ogg Vorbis, not lossless masters.
WAV working assets are decoded from those sources at their original 44.1 kHz rate; conversion does not restore lost detail.
Edits: conservative RMS/peak gain, short silence trims for effects only, mono effects, 1 ms effect / 2 ms music boundary ramps.
Music keeps its full original loop frame count. Natural effect tails below -80 dB are retained with a 25 ms margin.
No vocals, purchases, or attribution UI are required. Original project clips are preserved.
See audio-preparation.json for per-file original source, gain, duration, measured RMS/peak and checksum.
