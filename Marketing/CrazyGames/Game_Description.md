# Toy Car Sort

## Short description

Sort a colorful pile of toy cars into matching collection shelves. Complete every display, finish the collection, and unlock the legendary golden car.

## Full description

Turn a colorful pile of miniature cars into a collection worth showing off. Pick up a toy car, find its matching shelf, and place it in an empty slot. As the messy pile shrinks, neat rows of distinctive cars fill your displays.

Keep sorting through the different designs and themes until every car has found its place. Watch your collection take shape one satisfying placement at a time, then finish the entire collection to unlock the legendary golden car. Toy Car Sort is all about bringing order to the pile and enjoying the collection you create.
