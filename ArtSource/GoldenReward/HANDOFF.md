# Golden reward art handoff

## Assets and sources
The two verified main assets are Assets/TinyRiders/Art/GoldenReward/Prefabs/TR_GoldenCar_Reward.prefab and TR_GoldenReward_Display.prefab. TR_GoldenReward_Accessories is optional; TR_GoldenReward_Filled_ArtPreset nests the independent display and car. The actual Unity audit passed 34 checks with zero failures. PreviewScenes/TR_GoldenReward_Empty.unity and TR_GoldenReward_Filled.unity are saved static art previews.

Blender/GoldenReward_Master.blend contains the complete editable set and neutral studio. Blender/GoldenCar_Reward.blend is the separate car source. Blender/GoldenDisplay_Empty.blend is the independent empty display. File textures are packed into Blender and also supplied separately under Assets/TinyRiders/Art/GoldenReward/Textures. Production scripts are in Scripts; source meshes remain editable, while Unity meshes combine logical groups to reduce renderer overhead.

No supplier mesh was reused. The source-car mapping is NEW LOCAL MESH, guided by the two copied reference boards. The installed ithappy Car_01–Car_15 core meshes and actual material slots were inspected; source_audit.json and Renders/supplier_audit.png record that audit. The size comparison is Assets/TinyRiders/Gameplay/Compact/Prefabs/Held_TR-C001-TH_FIRE-V01.prefab, not the much larger authoring base prefab.

## Scale and integration points
Blender uses +Z up and -Y forward. FBX converts to Unity +Y up; the importer inspects the grille's actual forward position and corrects orientation if needed, then stores metric geometry below identity-scale roots. Car width is about 0.2001, length 0.3514, height 0.1590. Display diameter is 0.5409 and height about 0.4281. These are game-space units derived from the existing 0.35-long compact collectible, not the inconsistent concept-sheet cm labels.

Anchor_Car is under TurntablePivot, at the platter contact plane, approximately +0.054091 Unity Y. Attach the golden car with local position/rotation zero and scale one. Platter, GlassCover, AccentEmission and optional accessories remain independently addressable. The cover remains real thin-cylinder geometry; its material uses low opacity for readability. The art prefabs contain no gameplay scripts or driving controller.

Gameplay integration is installed in Assets/00_Project/00_Scenes/Level.unity. The grand exhibit replaces the original narrow pedestal with a 3m-wide, 0.2m-high base at (500,0,-7.75), near the counter. A separate installation parent scales the display and nested car by five: car length 1.757m, station width 2.705m. Authored prefab transforms remain intact; a presentation pivot beneath Anchor_Car carries the cinematic rise and rotation. No placard, reward text, carpet or ropes are installed. A scene material override gives the existing cover subtle Fresnel edges without reflected duplicate cars.

GoldenCarReveal keeps Complete(bool), ResetDisplay(), and keepCoverVisible=true. Its GoldenRewardCeremony drives a 90-second, six-phase energy ceremony, with camera shots, a 0.32m rise, one full rotation, lights, existing audio, and nine project-owned Polygon Arsenal effect variants. The cached publisher package and its Unity 6 URP upgrade were imported. A legacy PolySolidIntense material left by that upgrade is corrected only in the project's effect variants. Before completion the station is empty; completed saves show the settled car without replay. Existing correct-sorting rules and StoreCompleted remain authoritative, with no save-format changes. The exhibit exclusion and relocated pile poses protect walking routes; obstructing world cars from older saves recover to safe authored poses. The reward remains decorative.

Current validation and actual gameplay captures are recorded in IntegrationEvidence/GoldenReward/README.md and GrandCeremony/. Tests use isolated save keys; replay tests back up and restore upgrade preferences. The full-length visual test runs the actual 90-second reveal; lifecycle tests use four seconds. Browser/device performance has not been measured.

## Artwork and materials
Artwork/Checker_Master.svg and Checker_Master.png are editable seamless pattern sources. Artwork/Crown_Master.svg and Crown_Profile.json share the same crown silhouette used for geometric relief badges. Artwork/Grille_Master.svg creates the grille microdetail derivative. No checker square, text glyph, reflection stripe or sparkle is modeled as geometry. The hood checker faces belong to the actual body mesh; rocker, wing inset and circular fascia have analytic UV coordinates. Every mesh has an editable UV layer.

TR_Checker_BaseColor.png and TR_Grille_BaseColor.png are 512 × 512 RGB, sRGB base-color maps. The texel needs of these repeating motifs do not require 2048-square mostly empty atlases. Metallic and roughness are material constants: Unity smoothness = 1 minus Blender roughness. Gold uses metallic .88 / smoothness .77; edge gold .83 / .81. Rubber remains nonmetallic with smoothness .40. Windows are dark opaque material. Light accents use restrained emission. TR_StudioReflection.asset is a generated 64-square-per-face HDR cubemap used only for local preview reflections, not a baked car texture.

The transparent cover is deliberately simpler in Unity: alpha .025, no shadow casting, backface culling, specular/environment reflections disabled, with a separate gold rim. Blender mixes a mostly transparent shader with a small physical glass contribution. The reference's strong refractive reflections were reduced after an inspected render showed duplicate-looking car reflections. Emissive URP materials retain BakedEmissive flags so the installed shader validator keeps the emission keyword; no bake is required or performed.

## Reproduction
Run Scripts/make_artwork.py with local Python/Pillow. Run build_reward.py in installed Blender 5.1 background mode, followed by render_reward.py (arguments `-- initial`, then `-- final`) and save_sources.py. Run ImportGoldenReward.Main through the live Unity run_script tool once the Editor is stopped and available. Do not run any whole-collection generator. Existing .meta GUIDs in the owned subtree are retained by AssetDatabase updates.

Renders prefixed `unity_` are generated by actual Unity cameras. Other PNGs are Blender renders or clearly labelled audit/fit/contact sheets. The fit view intentionally hides the canopy to reveal the circular envelope. Preview lights/reflections are scene-local; no global URP, quality or post-processing assets are changed.
