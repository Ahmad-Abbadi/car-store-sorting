Authoring and rebuilding:
Run Blender 5.1 with --background --threads 8 --python ArtSource/UpgradeStation/Blender/build_factory.py.
This recreates the editable part-based source, globally unwraps meshes, bakes 2048 maps,
reduces the export mesh, converts its handedness, packs textures into the export .blend,
and exports FBX plus PNGs to ArtSource/UpgradeStation/Blender/Export.
Then run the Unity rebuild menu. It imports only FBX, base color, tangent normal, AO,
and metallic/smoothness textures into Factory/Blender and creates the URP material.
Metallic is R, smoothness is A; normal and AO use linear sampling. Source roughness and
metallic bakes remain available for editing. The .blend export contains packed images.
The construction .blend retains individually named beveled pieces and procedural materials.

Design and provenance:
All active housing, tool arms, hinges, hoses, controls, turntable, arrow badge, checker marks,
and racing graphics are newly authored in Blender for this project. Surfaces use baked
procedural enamel variation, rubber grain, brushed metal, normals and ambient occlusion.
The car reuses selected decorative meshes/materials from the existing ithappy asset:
Assets/ithappy/Modular_Racing_Cars/Prefabs/Car_01/Car_01.prefab.
No vehicle scripts, physics or gameplay behavior are copied.
Completion audio/VFX and the existing presentation controller are preserved.
No new paid asset was purchased.

Previously imported POLYGON Sci-Fi City mechanical parts remain available in the project,
but the new Blender arms replace them in the active machine. Their Synty license still applies
to those retained third-party source assets. The old source recipe is archived as
Blender/PreviousFactoryBuilder.cs.txt and is no longer the rebuild implementation.
