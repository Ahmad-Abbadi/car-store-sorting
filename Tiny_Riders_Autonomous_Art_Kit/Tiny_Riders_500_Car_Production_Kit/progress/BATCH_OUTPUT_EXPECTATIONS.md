# Expected Evidence for Every Production Batch

For each car or family batch, demand:

1. Source Blender scene and deterministic build recipe.
2. Real 3/4, side, front, rear, top, black silhouette and clay renders from the actual model.
3. Texture source artwork (editable 2D) and flat PNG atlas plus UV overlay.
4. Custom physical mesh variant comparison if applicable.
5. Car name/ID/rarity from catalog and printed label proof.
6. Correct flat card front/back textures, exact same car hero art; same package geometry and fixed layout as master.
7. Transparent physical blister close-up and box/wireframe audit.
8. Real `.fbx` or approved importer format and material-hookup manifest.
9. Actual QC script results and human-readable open issues.
10. 'STOP FOR REVIEW' state; no hidden next-batch generation.
