## Conflicts and resolution
- Generated concept boards vary in brand lettering, positions, product numbers, impossible small print, and actual number of lineup vehicles. **Do NOT mix their layouts**. Use the Track Club package composition as the universal grid, while changing the family color and flat theme graphics only.
- Some references display variant car bodies inconsistent with featured angles. A single approved 3D master model becomes the authority for ALL renders and package art.
- Printed hero car on card is a separate **2D render of the correct exact 3D car**; a second depicted car must not appear behind the real packaged car.
- General reference pack and crops support style but are not direct UV textures.
