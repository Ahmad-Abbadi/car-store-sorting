# 13 — Concept Boards, Editable Templates, and Finished Packaging Are Different

The images in `references/original_boards/` are the exact images already generated in this conversation. They are **concept sheets**, which include some inconsistent AI-generated lettering and layout differences. They are excellent stylistic refs but are NOT model orthographic drawings and cannot directly be used as production car-card textures.

The kit includes `design/packaging/svg/` family front/back **source templates** and `design/packaging/png_templates/` preview exports. These are real editable 2D bases derived from the intended visual language and common layout. Their upper hero art is deliberately empty because the **real, approved 3D car does not exist yet**. A blank hero zone is safer than letting the AI paste a false car into the packaging.

Only P03 and P08, after an approved 3D car exists, may create a *finished print artwork master* by compositing actual car renders with labels onto the correct source template. The real blister itself belongs in 3D. The source `MASTER_FRONT_LAYOUT_GUIDE.png` includes colored region boxes for visual comparison; **do not use that guide PNG as a packaging texture**.

'Exact same as reference' is operationalized as: same distinctive original brand treatment, same visual hierarchy, same aspect ratio/3D blister position, same controlled family palette and 2D background motif, and actual car appearance. Where the generated boards contradict one another, the standardized measured Track Club-derived master is the final technical contract. Have the owner explicitly approve master before expansion.
